We Bless This Mess is a solo project of Nelson Graf Reis, that Live is accompanied with band. Known as tattoo artist by some, known by many others as a member of the band Blackjackers, Nelson moves by his passions.
After the chaos and adventure in a Punk universe, WBTM arises, new project created by the need to explore the premise "less is more" and the desire to externalize the positivity and the carpe diem - "We breathe, we are alive. This is the greatest gift."- Nelson tells us.
